# Ex-4: Scenario-Based Report Development Utilizing Diverse Prompting Techniques

## 📘 Overview
This experiment focuses on designing an AI-powered chatbot capable of assisting customers in a retail environment.  
The goal is to utilize **diverse prompting techniques** to analyze how the chatbot performs in different real-life scenarios such as order tracking, refund requests, shipping delays, product availability, and customer complaints.

This README summarizes the **experiment design, methodology, prompts used, outputs, and analysis** as part of the lab requirement.

---

## 🎯 Objective
To develop a scenario-based retail support chatbot and explore how prompting techniques affect:
- Response quality  
- Reasoning depth  
- Tone consistency  
- Customer satisfaction  
- Accuracy in understanding user intent  

This helps in understanding how **prompt engineering** improves AI-driven customer support systems.

---

## 🎯 Aim
The aim of this experiment is:
- To apply AI prompting techniques for chatbot development  
- To simulate real customer service scenarios  
- To evaluate the chatbot's ability to handle various query types  
- To document observations in a structured report format  

The experiment helps students understand how AI language models can be adapted for practical, industry-based applications.

---

## 🔁 Algorithm / Methodology
1. **Problem Identification**  
   Select commonly occurring retail customer service scenarios.

2. **Data Collection**  
   Gather sample customer conversations, complaints, and FAQs.

3. **Scenario Design**  
   Prepare situations like order delay, refund issues, product info, etc.

4. **Prompt Engineering**  
   Apply multiple prompting techniques:
   - Zero-shot  
   - Few-shot  
   - Chain-of-thought reasoning  
   - Persona-based  
   - Scenario-based  

5. **Chatbot Response Generation**  
   Generate outputs using AI for each prompting style.

6. **Evaluation**  
   Rate responses based on:
   - Clarity  
   - Completeness  
   - Tone quality  
   - Customer satisfaction  

7. **Documentation**  
   Prepare a structured report with results and analysis.

---

## 🧩 Prompting Techniques Used (Detailed)

### ✅ 1. Zero-Shot Prompting
- No examples provided.  
- Chatbot responds only using instructions.  
- Useful for simple queries.  

### ✅ 2. Few-Shot Prompting
- Multiple example interactions included.  
- Helps chatbot maintain consistency across similar questions.  

### ✅ 3. Chain-of-Thought Prompting
- Forces the chatbot to think step by step.  
- Best for reasoning-heavy issues such as refunds or complaints.  

### ✅ 4. Persona Prompting
- Chatbot is instructed to act like a **professional retail support agent**.  
- Ensures polite tone, empathy, and professionalism.  

### ✅ 5. Scenario Prompting
- Full situation is described to generate more accurate responses.  
- Best for complex customer problems.

---


## 🟢 Result

The experiment successfully demonstrated the impact of various AI prompting techniques on chatbot performance in a retail environment. By applying zero-shot, few-shot, persona, scenario-based, and chain-of-thought prompting, the chatbot was able to:

- Provide accurate and relevant responses across multiple customer scenarios  
- Maintain a polite, professional, and empathetic tone suitable for customer service  
- Show improved reasoning and explanation quality when chain-of-thought prompting was used  
- Deliver consistent responses for similar queries when trained using few-shot examples  
- Adapt its personality and communication style with persona prompting  
- Handle complex customer problems more effectively with scenario-based prompting  

Overall, the study concludes that **prompt engineering plays a crucial role in enhancing chatbot performance**, improving user experience, and enabling AI systems to function reliably in real-world support situations.  
This experiment highlights how the choice of prompting technique directly influences the chatbot’s clarity, tone, reasoning ability, and customer satisfaction level.
